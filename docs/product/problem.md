# Problem: evidence work is hard to follow

## Context

Journalists, editors and fact-checkers often receive claims detached from their original source. Information can spread through reposts, messaging and social platforms before it reaches editorial review.

## User need

People responsible for verification need to trace claims to sources, compare independent accounts, identify contradictions and gaps, and explain uncertainty before making an editorial decision.

## Product direction

Agent 0 is an AI-assisted verification workspace intended to organize that investigative work. Its principles are “Assume nothing. Follow the evidence.” and “AI investigates. Humans decide.”

The product direction and initial audience come from the supplied project brief and brand references. This document does not claim validated user research, customer adoption, measured performance improvements or current product capabilities beyond the prototype in the repository.
