# Brag Plan: Agent 0

## What is this app?
Agent 0 helps people examine claims, public links, and images by organizing available evidence, what remains unknown, and what to check next.

## The angle
The video turns a familiar newsroom question into a visible evidence trail: a claim arrives, its sources are checked for independence, and uncertainty stays attached to the result. The centerpiece is the site’s own Nyeri schools example, shown throughout as illustrative rather than a live investigation. The payoff is a practical promise about judgment, not a promise that AI can decide what is true.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
“Know what holds up before you publish.” One line of outcome-first copy from the homepage lands over a dark editorial field, with the segmented 0-ring motif assembling into a quiet source trail.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A claim card uses the homepage’s exact sample: “Schools in Nyeri County will remain closed tomorrow.” It is explicitly labeled “Illustrative example · not a live check.”
- Three source findings appear as a traceable set: no matching official announcement found; some reporting repeats the same notice; the original image publisher is not established.
- The result remains “Not verified,” followed by the homepage’s explanation that there is not enough reliable evidence to confirm the announcement.

## Outro / punchline
“Assume nothing. Follow the evidence.” Then: “AI investigates. Humans decide.” Close on the Agent 0 wordmark and “Explore Agent 0.”

## User flow worth showing
Question/claim → review source findings and image-origin limits → read an explicitly illustrative, not-verified result. The public investigation entry is currently a demo/placeholder surface, so the video does not simulate a live submission or claim that this sample was actually checked.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: investigative newsroom ident, with the measured confidence of an editorial title sequence
- Interpretation: deliberate cuts, high-contrast type, one clean evidence reveal, and enough time to read each conclusion.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 23.4 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: charcoal `#0B0D0E`, with paper panels `#F7F7F4`
- Accent: signal green `#A3FF47`; amber `#F59E0B` only for the “Not verified” state
- Text: paper `#F7F7F4` on dark; charcoal `#0B0D0E` on paper
- Display font: system sans fallback (the project references Inter but does not ship a font file)
- Body font: system sans; technical labels use the system monospace stack (the project references JetBrains Mono but does not ship a font file)
- Strongest visual element: the segmented 0-ring/source-convergence motif, used once as quiet framing; the sample evidence panel and uncertainty language are the story.

## Share copy (draft)
Information moves fast. Verification takes work. Agent 0 keeps the claim, source trail, evidence and open questions together—so people can make the call.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained electronic interface soundscape using the user-supplied MP3.
- Music: none; the supplied file is titled “Futuristic interface | HUD sound effects,” so it is treated as the requested sound-design bed, not mislabeled as music.
- Music treatment: create a local playback mix from the supplied track at 20% gain with a short fade-in and fade-out; keep it beneath the words and result so its UI-like effects do not compete with the copy. Retain the original MP3 in the project.
- Music cue guidance: no bundled cue preset applies. The supplied file is effects-led rather than a musical beat track; use natural scene timing for readability and do not imply beat sync.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the existing segmented source ring breathe slightly with extracted RMS energy. No waveform, equalizer bars, or invented interface beeps.
- SFX posture: no added SFX; the requested track already carries interface effects.
- Audio-coupled moments: gentle bed fade-in at the opening, restrained swell as the source findings assemble, fade beneath the final wordmark.
- Restraint rule: keep the provided audio clearly subordinate to the spoken-free visual story; do not add narration or claim precise beat alignment.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Before publish — 3.0s
Dark charcoal frame, a restrained paper-white headline, and the segmented 0-ring assembling into a source path. Copy: “Know what holds up / before you publish.” The first screen establishes the audience outcome before naming features.
Sequential/interaction: the 0-ring segments assemble; the headline enters as two readable lines.
Audio intent: soft opening from the supplied HUD soundscape; no sharp transient under the hook.
Audio-coupled idea: the source ring gently responds to the existing sound level.
Music: none; supplied sound-design track only.
Transition mood: clean editorial cut → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The claim — 4.2s
A paper evidence card lands over charcoal. It shows the exact sample claim, with the small but legible label “ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE · NOT A LIVE CHECK.” No fictional upload, source count, or progress state is shown.
Sequential/interaction: the label and claim settle into a single sample card; no simulated live submission.
Audio intent: gentle low interface texture.
Audio-coupled idea: card arrival may align naturally with an existing soft transient if one is present.
Music: none; supplied sound-design track only.
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Follow the evidence — 6.3s
The claim stays visible in a smaller panel while three source findings connect to it one at a time: “No matching announcement found” (official sources); “Some reports repeat the same notice” (independent reporting); “Original publisher not established” (image origin). The illustration label remains present. Each item holds with all three visible together long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: 3 source rows enter in order and remain on screen; category labels stay attached to their findings.
Audio intent: quiet detail under a more active evidence reveal.
Audio-coupled idea: the row arrivals follow natural editorial timing, not a forced beat grid.
Music: none; supplied sound-design track only.
Transition mood: measured crossfade → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Keep uncertainty visible — 4.8s
A restrained amber status badge reads “NOT VERIFIED.” Supporting copy: “There isn’t enough reliable evidence to confirm this announcement.” A short second line reads “A missing source does not make a claim false.” The visual is a status with an explanation, not a truth score.
Sequential/interaction: status first, explanation follows; both remain settled and readable.
Audio intent: lower the soundscape so the assessment feels considered rather than triumphant.
Audio-coupled idea: natural timing; no success chime.
Music: none; supplied sound-design track only.
Transition mood: brief charcoal hold → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — People decide — 5.1s
Return to charcoal. The ring resolves once beside the Agent 0 wordmark. Copy: “Assume nothing. Follow the evidence.” and “AI investigates. Humans decide.” Small closing prompt: “Explore Agent 0.” Hold the complete lockup for at least two seconds.
Sequential/interaction: tagline, human-decision principle and wordmark settle in a single closing lockup.
Audio intent: fade the requested soundscape beneath the last line and finish cleanly.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle existing energy in the mark only; no fabricated sonic payoff.
Music: none; supplied sound-design track only.
Transition mood: final editorial hold.

**Music mood for this video:** restrained, investigative electronic texture from the supplied HUD effects track; no separate music bed.
**Audio summary:** the requested interface soundscape supports the evidence reveal at low gain, then fades under a calm human-centered close.

## Accuracy constraints
- Keep “Illustrative example · not a live check” readable anywhere the Nyeri claim or its findings appear.
- Do not imply that the claim was actually checked, that the school closure is true or false, or that Agent 0 has production availability.
- Do not invent numbers, customer proof, speed claims, endorsements, or a universal media-authenticity verdict.
- Keep “AI investigates. Humans decide.” attached to the close.
